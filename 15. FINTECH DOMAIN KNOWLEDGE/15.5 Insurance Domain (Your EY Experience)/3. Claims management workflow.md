# Claims Management Workflow — Interview Q&A (5 YOE Level)

---

## Section 1: Fundamentals

**Q1. What is a claim in the insurance/fintech context, and what problem does claims management solve?**
A: A claim is a formal request by a policyholder (or a third party on their behalf) to an insurer for compensation, reimbursement, or benefit payment under the terms of an insurance policy, following a covered loss event. Claims management is the end-to-end operational and technological process that governs how a claim is received, validated, investigated, adjudicated, and settled.

Claims management solves three distinct problems simultaneously:

- **Customer problem** — the policyholder has suffered a loss and needs compensation quickly, fairly, and with minimal friction. Poor claims experience is the single largest driver of customer churn in insurance.
- **Insurer problem** — the insurer must pay valid claims promptly while detecting and rejecting fraudulent or inflated claims, all within regulatory timelines and at minimum processing cost.
- **Regulatory problem** — IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India) mandates specific turnaround times, documentation requirements, grievance redressal procedures, and claim settlement ratios that insurers must meet or face penalties.

In Indian fintech, claims management is most prominent in:
- **General insurance** — motor, health, property, travel, device insurance (embedded insurance in fintech products).
- **Life insurance** — death claims, maturity claims, surrender claims, partial withdrawal.
- **Credit insurance / loan protection** — claim filed when a borrower dies, becomes disabled, or loses employment; the insurer pays outstanding loan balance to the lender.
- **Payment protection** — claim filed when a transaction is disputed as unauthorized; straddles insurance and payment chargeback.

**Q2. What are the phases of a claims management lifecycle, and who are the actors at each phase?**
A: The claims lifecycle has seven sequential phases, each with distinct actors and engineering touchpoints:

```
1. FNOL (First Notice of Loss)
   Actor: Policyholder, broker, hospital (in health)
   Channel: App, web, call center, WhatsApp, email
   Engineering: Intake API, FNOL form, document upload

2. Claim Registration
   Actor: Claims system (automated)
   Engineering: Claim number generation, policy validation,
                coverage check, initial triage

3. Document Collection
   Actor: Policyholder, surveyor, hospital, police
   Engineering: Document checklist engine, upload portal,
                OCR extraction, completeness check

4. Investigation / Survey
   Actor: Surveyor (motor/property), TPA (health), claims examiner
   Engineering: Surveyor assignment, field app, survey report upload,
                photo verification, fraud scoring

5. Adjudication
   Actor: Claims examiner, senior examiner, medical officer (health)
   Engineering: Coverage validation engine, deductible/copay calc,
                approval workflow, BRE (Business Rules Engine)

6. Settlement
   Actor: Finance team, payment system
   Engineering: Payment instruction generation, NEFT/IMPS/cheque,
                TDS deduction (if applicable), settlement letter

7. Closure and Feedback
   Actor: Policyholder, quality team
   Engineering: Claim closure, feedback collection, NPS,
                regulatory reporting
```

**Key actors:**
- **Policyholder** — files the claim; submits documents; receives settlement.
- **Surveyor / Loss Assessor** — licensed IRDAI surveyor who physically inspects damage (mandatory for claims above ₹75,000 in general insurance per IRDAI regulations).
- **TPA (Third Party Administrator)** — in health insurance, the TPA manages cashless claims between the insurer and hospitals; validates treatment eligibility; manages the hospital network.
- **Medical Officer** — reviews clinical documents in health claims; authorizes procedures in cashless hospitalization.
- **Reinsurer** — for large claims that exceed the insurer's retention limit, the reinsurer pays a portion; claims system must trigger reinsurance recovery.
- **IRDAI** — regulatory body that mandates timelines and receives statutory reports on claims settlement ratios and grievances.

**Q3. What is FNOL (First Notice of Loss), and what data must be captured at this stage?**
A: FNOL is the initial report filed by the policyholder notifying the insurer that a covered loss event has occurred. It starts the claims clock — regulatory timelines (IRDAI's 30-day settlement mandate for non-investigated claims) begin from FNOL. Getting FNOL right is critical: incomplete FNOL delays the entire downstream process.

**FNOL processing — what happens immediately after submission:**

```typescript
// claims/fnol/fnol.service.ts
@Injectable()
export class FnolService {
  async processFnol(request: FnolRequest): Promise<FnolResult> {
    // 1. Policy validation — does the policy exist and is it active?
    const policy = await this.policyService.validatePolicy(request.policyNumber);
    if (!policy) throw new PolicyNotFoundError(request.policyNumber);
    if (policy.status !== 'active') {
      throw new PolicyInactiveError(`Policy ${request.policyNumber} is ${policy.status}`);
    }

    // 2. Coverage check — is this loss type covered under the policy?
    const coverage = await this.coverageEngine.check({
      policy,
      lossType: request.lossType,
      lossDate: request.lossDate,
    });
    if (!coverage.isCovered) {
      // Register claim as 'repudiated' immediately — mandatory to notify claimant
      return this.registerRepudiatedClaim(request, coverage.repudiationReason);
    }

    // 3. Waiting period check
    if (coverage.inWaitingPeriod) {
      return this.registerRepudiatedClaim(request, 'loss_within_waiting_period');
    }

    // 4. Duplicate claim check — same policy, same loss date, same loss type
    const duplicate = await this.claimsRepo.findDuplicate(
      request.policyNumber, request.lossDate, request.lossType,
    );
    if (duplicate) {
      throw new DuplicateClaimError(`Claim already registered: ${duplicate.claimNumber}`);
    }

    // 5. Generate claim number — format: CLM-YYYY-NNNNNN (sequential, unique)
    const claimNumber = await this.claimNumberGenerator.generate();

    // 6. Triage — determines claim track (fast-track vs standard vs complex)
    const track = await this.triageEngine.classify(request, policy, coverage);

    // 7. Assign surveyor if required (IRDAI: mandatory for claims > ₹75,000)
    let surveyorAssignment: SurveyorAssignment | null = null;
    if (this.requiresSurveyor(request.lossType, request.estimatedLossAmount)) {
      surveyorAssignment = await this.surveyorService.assign(claimNumber, request.lossLocation);
    }

    // 8. Create claim record
    const claim = await this.claimsRepo.create({
      claimNumber,
      policyNumber: request.policyNumber,
      policyId: policy.id,
      claimantId: await this.claimantService.resolveOrCreate(request),
      lossType: request.lossType,
      lossDate: request.lossDate,
      lossLocation: request.lossLocation,
      lossDescription: request.lossDescription,
      estimatedLossAmount: request.estimatedLossAmount,
      status: 'registered',
      track,
      channel: request.channel,
      fnolReceivedAt: new Date(),
      surveyorAssignment,
      documentChecklist: await this.checklistEngine.generate(request.lossType, policy),
    });

    // 9. Trigger downstream events
    await this.eventBus.publish('claim.registered', {
      claimNumber,
      policyNumber: request.policyNumber,
      lossType: request.lossType,
      track,
    });

    // 10. Send acknowledgement to claimant (IRDAI mandates acknowledgement within 3 days)
    await this.notificationService.sendFnolAcknowledgement(claim);

    return { claimNumber, track, documentChecklist: claim.documentChecklist, surveyorAssignment };
  }

  private requiresSurveyor(lossType: LossType, estimatedAmount?: number): boolean {
    const ALWAYS_SURVEY = new Set(['motor_accident', 'property_damage']);
    if (ALWAYS_SURVEY.has(lossType)) return true;
    // IRDAI Surveyor and Loss Assessors Regulations: mandatory for claims > ₹75,000
    return (estimatedAmount ?? 0) > 75_000;
  }
}
```

**Q4. What is claims triage, and how does it determine the processing track?**
A: Claims triage is the automated initial classification of a claim into a processing track based on complexity, estimated value, loss type, fraud risk score, and policy characteristics. Getting triage right reduces cost and improves turnaround — simple claims should never wait behind complex ones in a single queue.

Three standard tracks:

**Fast-track (Straight-Through Processing — STP):**
- Low-value, low-complexity, low-fraud-risk claims.
- Fully automated adjudication — no human reviewer.
- Examples: travel delay claim with e-ticket proof, small device damage claim with purchase invoice and photo.
- Target turnaround: 1–4 hours.
- Criteria: estimated loss < ₹10,000, no fraud flags, complete documents at FNOL, no exclusion ambiguity.

**Standard track:**
- Medium complexity; human examiner reviews but with automation assistance.
- Examples: motor accident claim ₹50,000–₹2,00,000, health hospitalization with discharge summary.
- Target turnaround: 7–15 days.
- Criteria: surveyor required, standard documents needed, moderate fraud score.

**Complex track:**
- High-value, high-complexity, or suspected fraud.
- Multi-level review; may involve legal, medical officer, reinsurer notification.
- Examples: death claims, large property damage, claims with fraud indicators, claims involving litigation.
- Target turnaround: 30–90 days (within IRDAI limits).
- Criteria: estimated loss > ₹5,00,000, multiple loss events, prior claims history, fraud score above threshold, coverage ambiguity.

```typescript
// claims/triage/triage-engine.service.ts
@Injectable()
export class TriageEngine {
  async classify(
    fnol: FnolRequest,
    policy: Policy,
    coverage: CoverageResult,
  ): Promise<ClaimTrack> {
    const signals: TriageSignal[] = await Promise.all([
      this.scoreAmount(fnol.estimatedLossAmount, coverage.sumInsured),
      this.scoreComplexity(fnol.lossType, policy.productType),
      this.scoreFraud(fnol, policy),
      this.scoreDocumentCompleteness(fnol.initialDocuments, fnol.lossType),
      this.scoreClaimantHistory(policy.policyHolderId),
      this.scoreCoverageAmbiguity(fnol.lossType, coverage),
    ]);

    const weightedScore = this.computeWeightedScore(signals);

    // Mandatory complex-track overrides (regardless of score)
    if (await this.isMandatoryComplex(fnol, policy)) return 'complex';

    if (weightedScore <= 25) return 'fast_track';
    if (weightedScore <= 65) return 'standard';
    return 'complex';
  }

  private async isMandatoryComplex(fnol: FnolRequest, policy: Policy): Promise<boolean> {
    return (
      fnol.lossType === 'death' ||                          // all death claims are complex
      (fnol.estimatedLossAmount ?? 0) > 500_000 ||         // high-value mandatory complex
      policy.claimCount > 2 ||                             // 3rd+ claim — pattern review
      await this.sanctionsList.isPresent(fnol.claimantName) // OFAC/domestic sanctions
    );
  }
}
```

**Q5. What are the key IRDAI regulatory requirements that directly shape claims system design?**
A: IRDAI regulations are not optional guidelines — they are enforceable mandates with financial penalties and public disclosure requirements (claim settlement ratios published annually). The ones with the most direct engineering implications:

**IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, 2017 — key timelines:**

| Obligation | Timeline | Engineering Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Claim acknowledgement | Within 3 days of FNOL | Automated acknowledgement on claim creation |
| Document requirement communication | Within 15 days of FNOL | Document checklist must be generated and sent within 15 days |
| Claim decision (non-investigated) | Within 30 days of receiving complete documents | SLA timer starts when all docs are marked complete |
| Claim decision (investigated) | Within 45 days of appointing surveyor | Surveyor SLA tracking required |
| Repudiation communication | Within 30 days of decision | Repudiation letter with specific reason mandatory |
| Grievance resolution | Within 15 days | Grievance management system with SLA alerts |
| Interest on delayed claims | 2% per annum above bank rate on delayed settlement | Automated interest calculation triggers on SLA breach |

**IRDAI Surveyor Regulations:**
- Surveyors must be IRDAI-licensed; system must validate surveyor license before assignment.
- Surveyor report must be submitted within 30 days of appointment.
- Survey fee is regulated; must be paid within 30 days of report submission.

**IRDAI Motor Insurance Service Centre (MISP) requirements:**
- For motor claims, garages in the insurer's network must be geo-tagged; claim system must surface nearest network garage to claimant.

**Claim settlement ratio (CSR) reporting:**
- Insurers must file CSR with IRDAI annually; the data comes from the claims system. Engineering must ensure every claim disposition is correctly tagged (settled, repudiated, pending) with accurate amounts.

```typescript
// claims/sla/sla-monitor.service.ts
@Injectable()
export class SlаMonitorService {
  // Cron: every 30 minutes — check all open claims against SLA
  @Cron('*/30 * * * *')
  async checkSlaBreaches(): Promise<void> {
    const openClaims = await this.claimsRepo.findOpenClaims();

    for (const claim of openClaims) {
      const slaDeadlines = this.computeSlaDeadlines(claim);
      const now = new Date();

      for (const [milestone, deadline] of Object.entries(slaDeadlines)) {
        if (deadline && !claim.milestones[milestone] && now > deadline) {
          await this.handleSlaBreach(claim, milestone as SlaMilestone, deadline);
        }

        // Pre-breach warning: 24h before deadline
        const warningTime = subHours(deadline, 24);
        if (deadline && !claim.milestones[milestone] && now > warningTime && now < deadline) {
          await this.alertingService.sendSlaWarning(claim, milestone as SlaMilestone, deadline);
        }
      }
    }
  }

  private computeSlaDeadlines(claim: Claim): Record<string, Date | null> {
    const fnol = claim.fnolReceivedAt;
    const docsComplete = claim.documentsCompleteAt;
    const surveyorAppointed = claim.surveyorAppointedAt;

    return {
      acknowledgement:        addDays(fnol, 3),
      document_checklist:     addDays(fnol, 15),
      claim_decision:         docsComplete ? addDays(docsComplete, 30) : null,
      surveyor_decision:      surveyorAppointed ? addDays(surveyorAppointed, 45) : null,
      surveyor_report:        surveyorAppointed ? addDays(surveyorAppointed, 30) : null,
      settlement_payment:     claim.approvedAt ? addDays(claim.approvedAt, 7) : null,
    };
  }

  private async handleSlaBreach(
    claim: Claim,
    milestone: SlaMilestone,
    deadline: Date,
  ): Promise<void> {
    // Calculate interest penalty (IRDAI: 2% p.a. above bank rate on delayed settlement)
    if (milestone === 'settlement_payment') {
      const daysDelayed = differenceInDays(new Date(), deadline);
      const interestRate = (this.config.rbiBaseRate + 2) / 100 / 365;
      const penaltyAmount = claim.approvedAmount * interestRate * daysDelayed;
      await this.claimsRepo.recordInterestPenalty(claim.id, penaltyAmount);
    }

    await this.alertingService.escalateSlaBreach(claim, milestone);
    await this.auditLogRepo.record({
      claimId: claim.id,
      event: 'sla_breach',
      milestone,
      deadline,
      detectedAt: new Date(),
    });
  }
}
```

## Section 2: Document Management and Verification In Depth

**Q6. How do you design a dynamic document checklist engine for multiple insurance product lines?**
A: Different loss types require different documents. A motor accident claim needs a police FIR, driving licence, RC book, and repair estimate; a health hospitalization needs a discharge summary, bills, and prescriptions; a death claim needs a death certificate and nominee KYC. A hardcoded checklist per product line is unmaintainable — a rules-driven checklist engine is the correct approach.

```typescript
// claims/documents/checklist-engine.service.ts
export interface DocumentRule {
  documentType: DocumentType;
  label: string;
  mandatory: boolean;
  condition?: DocumentCondition;    // optional — document required only if condition is true
  maxAgeDays?: number;              // e.g., FIR must be filed within 24h of incident
  acceptedFormats: string[];        // ['pdf', 'jpg', 'png']
  maxSizeMb: number;
}

export interface DocumentCondition {
  field: string;                    // e.g., 'estimatedLossAmount'
  operator: '>' | '<' | '==' | 'in';
  value: unknown;
}

@Injectable()
export class ChecklistEngine {

      { documentType: 'rc_book', label: 'Registration Certificate (RC Book)', mandatory: true, acceptedFormats: ['pdf', 'jpg', 'png'], maxSizeMb: 5 },
      { documentType: 'police_fir', label: 'Police FIR / MLC Report', mandatory: true, maxAgeDays: 3, acceptedFormats: ['pdf'], maxSizeMb: 10 },

      { documentType: 'repair_invoice', label: 'Final Repair Invoice', mandatory: false, acceptedFormats: ['pdf'], maxSizeMb: 10 },  // required at settlement
      { documentType: 'accident_photos', label: 'Photographs of Damaged Vehicle', mandatory: true, acceptedFormats: ['jpg', 'png'], maxSizeMb: 20 },
      { documentType: 'fitness_certificate', label: 'Fitness Certificate', mandatory: false,
        condition: { field: 'vehicleType', operator: 'in', value: ['commercial', 'bus', 'truck'] },
        acceptedFormats: ['pdf', 'jpg'], maxSizeMb: 5,
      },
    ],
    health_hospitalization: [
      { documentType: 'claim_form', label: 'Duly Signed Claim Form', mandatory: true, acceptedFormats: ['pdf'], maxSizeMb: 5 },
      { documentType: 'discharge_summary', label: 'Discharge Summary', mandatory: true, acceptedFormats: ['pdf'], maxSizeMb: 10 },

      { documentType: 'prescription', label: 'Doctor\'s Prescription', mandatory: true, acceptedFormats: ['pdf', 'jpg'], maxSizeMb: 10 },
      { documentType: 'diagnostic_reports', label: 'Lab / Diagnostic Reports', mandatory: true, acceptedFormats: ['pdf', 'jpg'], maxSizeMb: 20 },

      { documentType: 'death_certificate', label: 'Original Death Certificate (issued by Municipal Authority)', mandatory: true, acceptedFormats: ['pdf'], maxSizeMb: 5 },
      { documentType: 'nominee_kyc', label: 'KYC Documents of Nominee / Legal Heir', mandatory: true, acceptedFormats: ['pdf', 'jpg', 'png'], maxSizeMb: 10 },
      { documentType: 'nominee_bank_details', label: 'Cancelled Cheque of Nominee\'s Bank Account', mandatory: true, acceptedFormats: ['pdf', 'jpg'], maxSizeMb: 5 },
      { documentType: 'medical_records', label: 'Medical Records (last 3 years)', mandatory: false,
        condition: { field: 'deathCause', operator: 'in', value: ['illness', 'natural', 'unknown'] },
        acceptedFormats: ['pdf'], maxSizeMb: 50,
      },
      { documentType: 'post_mortem_report', label: 'Post-Mortem Report', mandatory: false,
        condition: { field: 'deathCause', operator: 'in', value: ['accident', 'unnatural'] },
        acceptedFormats: ['pdf'], maxSizeMb: 10,
      },
      { documentType: 'succession_certificate', label: 'Succession Certificate / Legal Heir Certificate', mandatory: false,
        condition: { field: 'nomineeAvailable', operator: '==', value: false },
        acceptedFormats: ['pdf'], maxSizeMb: 10,
      },
    ],
    // ... other loss types
  };

  async generate(lossType: LossType, policy: Policy, context?: ClaimContext): Promise<DocumentChecklist> {
    const rules = this.CHECKLIST_RULES[lossType] ?? [];

    const applicableRules = rules.filter(rule => {
      if (!rule.condition) return true;
      return this.evaluateCondition(rule.condition, context ?? {});
    });

    return {
      lossType,
      documents: applicableRules.map(rule => ({
        documentType: rule.documentType,
        label: rule.label,
        mandatory: rule.mandatory,
        status: 'pending',
        acceptedFormats: rule.acceptedFormats,
        maxSizeMb: rule.maxSizeMb,
        maxAgeDays: rule.maxAgeDays,
      })),
      generatedAt: new Date(),
      allMandatoryDocumentsReceived: false,
    };
  }

  private evaluateCondition(condition: DocumentCondition, context: Record<string, unknown>): boolean {
    const fieldValue = context[condition.field];
    switch (condition.operator) {
      case '>':  return (fieldValue as number) > (condition.value as number);
      case '<':  return (fieldValue as number) < (condition.value as number);
      case '==': return fieldValue === condition.value;
      case 'in': return (condition.value as unknown[]).includes(fieldValue);
      default:   return false;
    }
  }
}
```

**Q7. How do you implement document upload, OCR extraction, and completeness tracking?**
A: Document management in claims is a three-stage pipeline: ingest (upload + virus scan + format validation), extract (OCR + field extraction + quality scoring), and validate (field-level business rules + completeness check).

```typescript
// claims/documents/document.service.ts
@Injectable()
export class DocumentService {
  async uploadDocument(
    claimId: string,
    documentType: DocumentType,
    file: Express.Multer.File,
    uploadedBy: string,
  ): Promise<DocumentUploadResult> {
    // Stage 1: Ingest
    await this.validateFileFormat(file, documentType);
    await this.scanForMalware(file);  // ClamAV / cloud AV scan

    // Upload to S3 with server-side encryption (AES-256)
    const s3Key = `claims/${claimId}/documents/${documentType}/${generateUUID()}.${this.getExtension(file)}`;
    await this.s3.putObject({
      Bucket: this.config.documentsBucket,
      Key: s3Key,
      Body: file.buffer,
      ContentType: file.mimetype,
      ServerSideEncryption: 'AES256',
      Metadata: {
        claimId,
        documentType,
        uploadedBy,
        uploadedAt: new Date().toISOString(),
      },
    });

    // Create document record in DB
    const document = await this.documentRepo.create({
      claimId,
      documentType,
      s3Key,
      originalFilename: file.originalname,
      fileSizeBytes: file.size,
      mimeType: file.mimetype,
      status: 'uploaded',
      uploadedBy,
    });

    // Stage 2: Async OCR + extraction (via SQS → Lambda)
    await this.ocrQueue.enqueue({
      documentId: document.id,
      claimId,
      documentType,
      s3Key,
    });

    // Stage 3: Update checklist completeness
    await this.updateChecklistStatus(claimId, documentType, 'uploaded');

    return {
      documentId: document.id,
      status: 'uploaded',
      processingStatus: 'ocr_pending',
    };
  }

  // Called by OCR Lambda after processing
  async processOcrResult(
    documentId: string,
    ocrResult: OcrResult,
  ): Promise<void> {
    const document = await this.documentRepo.findById(documentId);

    // Quality check — is the document readable?
    if (ocrResult.qualityScore < 60) {
      await this.documentRepo.update(documentId, {
        status: 'quality_rejected',
        rejectionReason: 'Document is blurry or illegible. Please re-upload a clear image.',
      });
      await this.notifyQualityRejection(document.claimId, documentId);
      return;
    }

    // Extract and store structured fields
    const extractedFields = await this.fieldExtractor.extract(
      document.documentType,
      ocrResult.text,
    );

    await this.documentRepo.update(documentId, {
      status: 'processed',
      ocrText: ocrResult.text,
      extractedFields,
      qualityScore: ocrResult.qualityScore,
      processedAt: new Date(),
    });

    // Validate extracted fields against claim data
    await this.validateExtractedFields(document.claimId, document.documentType, extractedFields);

    // Update checklist — document is now 'received' not just 'uploaded'
    await this.updateChecklistStatus(document.claimId, document.documentType, 'received');

    // Check if all mandatory documents are now complete
    await this.checkDocumentCompleteness(document.claimId);
  }

  private async checkDocumentCompleteness(claimId: string): Promise<void> {
    const checklist = await this.checklistRepo.findByClaim(claimId);
    const mandatoryDocs = checklist.documents.filter(d => d.mandatory);
    const allReceived = mandatoryDocs.every(d => d.status === 'received');

    if (allReceived && !checklist.allMandatoryDocumentsReceived) {
      await this.checklistRepo.markComplete(claimId);
      await this.claimsRepo.update(claimId, { documentsCompleteAt: new Date() });

      // This event starts the 30-day settlement SLA clock
      await this.eventBus.publish('claim.documents_complete', { claimId });
    }
  }
}
```

## Section 3: Adjudication Engine In Depth

**Q8. What is claims adjudication, and how do you implement a rules-based adjudication engine?**
A: Adjudication is the process of evaluating a claim against the policy terms and applicable rules to determine: is the claim payable? If yes, how much? The adjudication engine is the core of claims intelligence — it applies coverage rules, exclusions, deductibles, copayments, sub-limits, and fraud signals to produce a payable amount and a recommendation (approve / reject / refer to examiner).

```typescript
// claims/adjudication/adjudication-engine.service.ts
export interface AdjudicationInput {
  claim: Claim;
  policy: Policy;
  coverage: CoverageDetails;
  surveyorReport?: SurveyorReport;
  documents: ProcessedDocument[];
  extractedLossAmount: number;     // from surveyor report or bills
}

export interface AdjudicationResult {
  decision: 'approve' | 'partial_approve' | 'reject' | 'refer_examiner';
  payableAmount?: number;
  rejectionReasons?: string[];
  deductions: Deduction[];
  referralReason?: string;
  confidence: number;             // 0–100; low confidence → refer to examiner
  appliedRules: AppliedRule[];    // audit trail of every rule that fired
}

export interface Deduction {
  type: 'deductible' | 'depreciation' | 'copay' | 'sub_limit' | 'non_payable_item' | 'salvage';
  amount: number;
  reason: string;
}

@Injectable()
export class AdjudicationEngine {
  async adjudicate(input: AdjudicationInput): Promise<AdjudicationResult> {
    const appliedRules: AppliedRule[] = [];
    const deductions: Deduction[] = [];
    let baseAmount = input.extractedLossAmount;

    // Rule 1: Exclusion check
    const exclusions = await this.checkExclusions(input.claim, input.policy, input.coverage);
    if (exclusions.length > 0) {
      appliedRules.push({ rule: 'exclusion_check', outcome: 'reject', detail: exclusions });
      return {
        decision: 'reject',
        rejectionReasons: exclusions.map(e => e.reason),
        deductions: [],
        appliedRules,
        confidence: 95,
      };
    }

    // Rule 2: Sum insured check
    if (baseAmount > input.coverage.sumInsured) {
      const excessAmount = baseAmount - input.coverage.sumInsured;
      deductions.push({ type: 'sub_limit', amount: excessAmount, reason: 'Exceeds sum insured' });
      baseAmount = input.coverage.sumInsured;
      appliedRules.push({ rule: 'sum_insured_cap', outcome: 'deducted', detail: { excessAmount } });
    }

    // Rule 3: Deductible (excess / compulsory deductible)
    if (input.coverage.compulsoryDeductible > 0) {
      const deductible = Math.min(input.coverage.compulsoryDeductible, baseAmount);
      deductions.push({ type: 'deductible', amount: deductible, reason: 'Compulsory deductible per policy terms' });
      baseAmount -= deductible;
      appliedRules.push({ rule: 'compulsory_deductible', outcome: 'deducted', detail: { deductible } });
    }

    // Voluntary deductible (if policyholder opted for higher deductible in exchange for lower premium)
    if (input.coverage.voluntaryDeductible > 0) {
      const volDeductible = Math.min(input.coverage.voluntaryDeductible, baseAmount);
      deductions.push({ type: 'deductible', amount: volDeductible, reason: 'Voluntary deductible per policy' });
      baseAmount -= volDeductible;
    }

    // Rule 4: Depreciation (for motor claims on non-zero-dep policies)
    if (input.claim.lossType === 'motor_accident' && !input.coverage.zeroDep) {
      const depAmount = await this.depreciationEngine.calculate(
        input.claim, input.surveyorReport, input.policy.vehicleAge,
      );
      deductions.push({ type: 'depreciation', amount: depAmount, reason: 'Depreciation as per IRDAI schedule' });
      baseAmount -= depAmount;
      appliedRules.push({ rule: 'depreciation', outcome: 'deducted', detail: { depAmount } });
    }

    // Rule 5: Sub-limits (health: room rent limit, specific procedure limits)
    if (input.claim.lossType === 'health_hospitalization') {
      const subLimitDeductions = await this.subLimitEngine.apply(
        input.claim, input.coverage, input.documents,
      );
      for (const d of subLimitDeductions) {
        deductions.push(d);
        baseAmount -= d.amount;
      }
    }

    // Rule 6: Co-payment (health: policyholder bears a % of eligible claim)
    if (input.coverage.copaymentPct > 0) {
      const copayAmount = Math.round(baseAmount * input.coverage.copaymentPct / 100);
      deductions.push({ type: 'copay', amount: copayAmount, reason: `${input.coverage.copaymentPct}% co-payment as per policy` });
      baseAmount -= copayAmount;
    }

    // Rule 7: Non-payable items (health: consumables, registration charges, etc.)
    if (input.claim.lossType === 'health_hospitalization') {
      const nonPayable = await this.nonPayableEngine.identify(input.documents);
      if (nonPayable.amount > 0) {
        deductions.push({ type: 'non_payable_item', amount: nonPayable.amount, reason: 'Non-payable items as per policy schedule' });
        baseAmount -= nonPayable.amount;
      }
    }

    const payableAmount = Math.max(0, Math.round(baseAmount * 100) / 100);

    // Confidence scoring — low confidence triggers human review
    const confidence = await this.confidenceScorer.score(input, appliedRules, deductions);

    if (confidence < 70) {
      return {
        decision: 'refer_examiner',
        payableAmount,
        deductions,
        referralReason: `Low confidence score (${confidence}). Recommend examiner review.`,
        appliedRules,
        confidence,
      };
    }

    return {
      decision: payableAmount > 0 ? 'approve' : 'reject',
      payableAmount,
      deductions,
      appliedRules,
      confidence,
    };
  }
}
```

**Q9. How do you implement a depreciation engine for motor insurance claims?**
A: In motor insurance, depreciation is applied to parts that are replaced (not repaired) when the policy does not have a "zero depreciation" add-on. IRDAI specifies the depreciation schedule in its Motor Insurance guidelines. Getting depreciation right is critical — it is the most common source of claim disputes in motor insurance.

**IRDAI depreciation schedule for motor parts:**

| Vehicle Age | Depreciation Rate on Metal/Fibre Parts |
|---|---|
| < 6 months | 0% |
| 6 months – 1 year | 5% |
| 1 – 2 years | 10% |
| 2 – 3 years | 15% |
| 3 – 4 years | 25% |
| 4 – 5 years | 35% |
| 5 – 10 years | 40% |
| > 10 years | 50% |

Rubber, nylon, plastic parts: 50% depreciation regardless of vehicle age. Glass parts: 0% depreciation. Tyres: 50%.

```typescript
// claims/adjudication/depreciation-engine.service.ts
export type PartCategory = 'metal_fibre' | 'rubber_plastic_nylon' | 'glass' | 'tyre' | 'battery';

export interface RepairLineItem {
  description: string;
  partCategory: PartCategory;
  action: 'repair' | 'replace';
  amount: number;
}

@Injectable()
export class DepreciationEngine {
  private readonly PART_DEPRECIATION_RATES: Record<PartCategory, number | null> = {
    metal_fibre: null,          // age-dependent — see schedule
    rubber_plastic_nylon: 50,
    glass: 0,
    tyre: 50,
    battery: 50,
  };

  private readonly AGE_BASED_RATES: Array<{ maxMonths: number; rate: number }> = [
    { maxMonths: 6,   rate: 0  },
    { maxMonths: 12,  rate: 5  },
    { maxMonths: 24,  rate: 10 },
    { maxMonths: 36,  rate: 15 },
    { maxMonths: 48,  rate: 25 },
    { maxMonths: 60,  rate: 35 },
    { maxMonths: 120, rate: 40 },
    { maxMonths: Infinity, rate: 50 },
  ];

  calculate(
    lineItems: RepairLineItem[],
    vehicleManufactureDate: Date,
  ): DepreciationCalculation {
    const vehicleAgeMonths = differenceInMonths(new Date(), vehicleManufactureDate);
    const ageDrivenRate = this.getAgeDrivenRate(vehicleAgeMonths);
    const breakdown: DepreciationLineItem[] = [];
    let totalDepreciation = 0;

    for (const item of lineItems) {
      // Depreciation applies only to replacement, not repair
      if (item.action === 'repair') {
        breakdown.push({ ...item, depreciationRate: 0, depreciationAmount: 0 });
        continue;
      }

      const rate = this.PART_DEPRECIATION_RATES[item.partCategory] ?? ageDrivenRate;
      const depAmount = Math.round(item.amount * rate / 100 * 100) / 100;
      totalDepreciation += depAmount;

      breakdown.push({
        description: item.description,
        partCategory: item.partCategory,
        action: item.action,
        amount: item.amount,
        depreciationRate: rate,
        depreciationAmount: depAmount,
        payableAmount: item.amount - depAmount,
      });
    }

    return {
      vehicleAgeMonths,
      ageDrivenDepreciationRate: ageDrivenRate,
      breakdown,
      totalDepreciation: Math.round(totalDepreciation * 100) / 100,
    };
  }

  private getAgeDrivenRate(vehicleAgeMonths: number): number {
    return this.AGE_BASED_RATES.find(r => vehicleAgeMonths <= r.maxMonths)?.rate ?? 50;
  }
}
```

**Q10. How do you implement health insurance sub-limit enforcement in the adjudication engine?**
A: Health insurance policies have numerous sub-limits — caps on specific items within the overall sum insured. Room rent limits are the most impactful and the most disputed. If the patient occupies a room above the entitled category, many insurers proportionately reduce the entire hospitalization bill (not just the room charges) — a rule that shocks policyholders.

**Room rent proportionate deduction rule:**

```typescript
// claims/adjudication/sub-limit-engine.service.ts
@Injectable()
export class SubLimitEngine {
  async apply(
    claim: Claim,
    coverage: CoverageDetails,
    documents: ProcessedDocument[],
  ): Promise<Deduction[]> {
    const deductions: Deduction[] = [];
    const hospitalBill = this.extractHospitalBill(documents);

    // Sub-limit 1: Room rent
    if (coverage.roomRentLimit && hospitalBill.roomRentPerDay > 0) {
      const roomRentDeductions = this.applyRoomRentLimit(
        hospitalBill, coverage.roomRentLimit,
      );
      deductions.push(...roomRentDeductions);
    }

    // Sub-limit 2: ICU charges
    if (coverage.icuLimit && hospitalBill.icuChargesPerDay > 0) {
      if (hospitalBill.icuChargesPerDay > coverage.icuLimit) {
        const excess = (hospitalBill.icuChargesPerDay - coverage.icuLimit) * hospitalBill.icuDays;
        deductions.push({ type: 'sub_limit', amount: excess, reason: `ICU charges exceed sub-limit of ₹${coverage.icuLimit}/day` });
      }
    }

    // Sub-limit 3: Specific procedures
    for (const procedure of hospitalBill.procedures) {
      const procedureLimit = coverage.procedureLimits?.[procedure.code];
      if (procedureLimit && procedure.chargedAmount > procedureLimit) {
        deductions.push({
          type: 'sub_limit',
          amount: procedure.chargedAmount - procedureLimit,
          reason: `${procedure.name} charges exceed sub-limit of ₹${procedureLimit}`,
        });
      }
    }

    return deductions;
  }

  private applyRoomRentLimit(
    bill: HospitalBill,
    roomRentLimit: RoomRentLimit,
  ): Deduction[] {
    const deductions: Deduction[] = [];
    const actualRoomRent = bill.roomRentPerDay;
    const entitledRoomRent = roomRentLimit.type === 'fixed_amount'
      ? roomRentLimit.amountPerDay
      : roomRentLimit.percentageOfSumInsured / 100 * bill.sumInsured / 365;

    if (actualRoomRent <= entitledRoomRent) return []; // no deduction

    // Room rent deduction on room charges
    const roomChargeExcess = (actualRoomRent - entitledRoomRent) * bill.roomDays;
    deductions.push({
      type: 'sub_limit',
      amount: roomChargeExcess,
      reason: `Room rent ${actualRoomRent}/day exceeds entitlement ${entitledRoomRent}/day`,
    });

    // Proportionate deduction on all other charges (the controversial rule)
    // Ratio = entitled_room_rent / actual_room_rent
    // Applied to: nursing, doctor visits, investigations, medicines (not room rent itself)
    if (roomRentLimit.applyProportionateDeduction) {
      const ratio = entitledRoomRent / actualRoomRent;
      const proportionateAmount = bill.otherCharges * (1 - ratio);
      deductions.push({
        type: 'sub_limit',
        amount: Math.round(proportionateAmount * 100) / 100,
        reason: `Proportionate deduction on other charges due to room upgrade (ratio: ${(ratio * 100).toFixed(1)}%)`,
      });
    }

    return deductions;
  }
}
```

## Section 4: Fraud Detection and Settlement In Depth

**Q11. How do you implement a fraud scoring engine for insurance claims?**
A: Insurance fraud accounts for an estimated 10–15% of all claims paid in India (IRDAI estimates). A fraud scoring engine runs at two points: at FNOL (to triage the claim) and at adjudication (before payment). It outputs a fraud risk score and a set of fraud indicators that the examiner must review.

```typescript
// claims/fraud/fraud-scoring.service.ts
export interface FraudScore {
  score: number;           // 0–100; > 70 = high risk; 40–70 = medium; < 40 = low
  riskLevel: 'low' | 'medium' | 'high';
  indicators: FraudIndicator[];
  recommendedAction: 'auto_process' | 'enhanced_review' | 'investigation';
}

export interface FraudIndicator {
  rule: string;
  detail: string;
  weight: number;           // contribution to overall score
}

@Injectable()
export class FraudScoringService {
  async score(claim: Claim, policy: Policy, documents: ProcessedDocument[]): Promise<FraudScore> {
    const indicators: FraudIndicator[] = [];

    // Signal 1: Claim frequency — multiple claims in short period
    const recentClaims = await this.claimsRepo.countRecentClaims(policy.policyHolderId, 365);
    if (recentClaims >= 3) {
      indicators.push({ rule: 'high_claim_frequency', detail: `${recentClaims} claims in last 12 months`, weight: 20 });
    }

    // Signal 2: Claim filed very soon after policy issuance
    const daysSincePolicyStart = differenceInDays(claim.lossDate, policy.startDate);
    if (daysSincePolicyStart < 30) {
      indicators.push({ rule: 'early_claim', detail: `Claim ${daysSincePolicyStart} days after policy start`, weight: 25 });
    }

    // Signal 3: Loss date = weekend or holiday (higher fraud rate empirically)
    if (isWeekend(claim.lossDate) || await this.calendarService.isHoliday(claim.lossDate)) {
      indicators.push({ rule: 'weekend_holiday_loss', detail: 'Loss reported on weekend/holiday', weight: 5 });
    }

    // Signal 4: Estimated loss close to sum insured
    if (claim.estimatedLossAmount && policy.sumInsured) {
      const ratio = claim.estimatedLossAmount / policy.sumInsured;
      if (ratio > 0.85) {
        indicators.push({ rule: 'high_loss_to_si_ratio', detail: `Estimated loss is ${(ratio * 100).toFixed(0)}% of SI`, weight: 15 });
      }
    }

    // Signal 5: Geo-inconsistency — loss location vs policyholder's registered address
    if (claim.geoLocation) {
      const distanceKm = this.geoService.distanceKm(claim.geoLocation, policy.registeredAddress);
      if (distanceKm > 500) {
        indicators.push({ rule: 'geo_inconsistency', detail: `Loss ${distanceKm.toFixed(0)}km from registered address`, weight: 10 });
      }
    }

    // Signal 6: Document inconsistencies — dates, amounts in extracted fields
    const docInconsistencies = await this.documentAnalyzer.checkConsistency(claim, documents);
    for (const inc of docInconsistencies) {
      indicators.push({ rule: 'document_inconsistency', detail: inc.detail, weight: inc.severity === 'high' ? 20 : 10 });
    }

    // Signal 7: Known fraudulent provider / garage / hospital
    if (claim.serviceProviderId) {
      const blacklisted = await this.providerRegistry.isBlacklisted(claim.serviceProviderId);
      if (blacklisted) {
        indicators.push({ rule: 'blacklisted_provider', detail: `Provider ${claim.serviceProviderId} is blacklisted`, weight: 40 });
      }
    }

    // Signal 8: Network analysis — policyholder connected to previously fraudulent claims
    const networkRisk = await this.networkAnalyzer.scoreConnections(policy.policyHolderId);
    if (networkRisk > 0.6) {
      indicators.push({ rule: 'fraud_network_connection', detail: `Network risk score: ${networkRisk.toFixed(2)}`, weight: 30 });
    }

    const totalScore = Math.min(100, indicators.reduce((sum, i) => sum + i.weight, 0));
    const riskLevel = totalScore >= 70 ? 'high' : totalScore >= 40 ? 'medium' : 'low';

    return {
      score: totalScore,
      riskLevel,
      indicators,
      recommendedAction:
        totalScore >= 70 ? 'investigation' :
        totalScore >= 40 ? 'enhanced_review' : 'auto_process',
    };
  }
}
```

**Q12. How do you design the settlement payment flow for an approved claim?**
A: Settlement is the final financial act — funds move from the insurer to the claimant. It must be accurate (correct amount after all deductions), compliant (TDS deducted where applicable), fast (within IRDAI's 7-day target after approval), and auditable.

```typescript
// claims/settlement/settlement.service.ts
@Injectable()
export class SettlementService {
  async initiateSettlement(
    claimId: string,
    approvedAmount: number,
    approvedBy: string,
  ): Promise<Settlement> {
    const claim = await this.claimsRepo.findById(claimId);
    const claimant = await this.claimantRepo.findById(claim.claimantId);

    // TDS calculation (Income Tax Act Section 194DA for life insurance maturity
    // claims where sum > ₹1L and premium > 10% of sum assured)
    const tds = await this.tdsEngine.calculate(claim, approvedAmount);

    const netPayableAmount = approvedAmount - tds.amount;

    // Validate bank account
    const bankAccount = await this.bankAccountRepo.findByClaimant(claim.claimantId);
    if (!bankAccount) throw new MissingBankDetailsError(claim.claimantId);
    await this.bankValidator.validate(bankAccount); // penny-drop or IMPS validation

    // Generate settlement record
    const settlement = await this.settlementRepo.create({
      claimId,
      claimantId: claim.claimantId,
      approvedAmount,
      tdsAmount: tds.amount,
      tdsRate: tds.rate,
      netPayableAmount,
      bankAccountId: bankAccount.id,
      status: 'pending',
      approvedBy,
      settlementMode: this.resolveSettlementMode(netPayableAmount),
      initiatedAt: new Date(),
      dueBy: addDays(new Date(), 7),  // IRDAI: 7 days from approval
    });

    // Create payment instruction
    const paymentInstruction = await this.paymentService.createInstruction({
      amount: netPayableAmount,
      currency: 'INR',
      beneficiaryName: claimant.name,
      beneficiaryAccount: bankAccount.accountNumber,
      beneficiaryIfsc: bankAccount.ifsc,
      mode: settlement.settlementMode,
      referenceId: settlement.id,
      remarks: `Claim settlement - ${claim.claimNumber}`,
      idempotencyKey: `settlement:${settlement.id}`,
    });

    await this.settlementRepo.update(settlement.id, {
      paymentInstructionId: paymentInstruction.id,
    });

    // Generate settlement letter (PDF — sent to claimant via email + courier)
    await this.settlementLetterService.generate(settlement, claim, claimant);

    // Update claim status
    await this.claimsRepo.update(claimId, {
      status: 'settlement_initiated',
      settlementId: settlement.id,
    });

    await this.eventBus.publish('claim.settlement_initiated', {
      claimId,
      settlementId: settlement.id,
      amount: netPayableAmount,
    });

    return settlement;
  }

  private resolveSettlementMode(amount: number): PaymentMode {
    if (amount <= 2_00_000) return 'imps';    // instant, 24x7
    if (amount <= 10_00_000) return 'neft';   // next working day
    return 'rtgs';                             // same day, large amounts
  }

  // Called by payment system webhook on payment status update
  async handlePaymentUpdate(
    settlementId: string,
    paymentStatus: 'success' | 'failed',
    paymentReference: string,
  ): Promise<void> {
    const settlement = await this.settlementRepo.findById(settlementId);

    if (paymentStatus === 'success') {
      await this.settlementRepo.update(settlementId, {
        status: 'paid',
        paymentReference,
        paidAt: new Date(),
      });
      await this.claimsRepo.update(settlement.claimId, {
        status: 'settled',
        settledAt: new Date(),
      });
      await this.notificationService.sendSettlementConfirmation(settlement);

    } else {
      await this.settlementRepo.update(settlementId, {
        status: 'payment_failed',
        failureReason: paymentReference,
        failedAt: new Date(),
      });
      // Trigger retry or manual intervention
      await this.alertingService.raiseSettlementFailure(settlementId);
    }
  }
}
```

**Q13. "Design the complete claims management system for an embedded insurance product in a fintech lending app — loan protection insurance."**
A: Loan protection insurance (also called credit life insurance) pays the outstanding loan balance to the lender when the borrower dies or becomes permanently disabled. Since it is embedded in a lending product, the claims trigger, beneficiary, and settlement are all integrated with the loan system.

```
Product: Loan Protection Insurance — embedded in personal loan
Covered events: Death, Permanent Total Disability (PTD)
Beneficiary: The NBFC (lender) — receives outstanding principal + interest
Claimant: Nominee of deceased borrower (files the claim; settlement goes to lender)

Integration architecture:

Loan Service ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │ loan_disbursed event                                                 │
    ▼                                                                      │
Policy Service                                                             │
    │ auto-issue policy (group policy — NBFC is master policyholder)       │
    │ coverage = outstanding loan amount (reduces with each EMI)           │
    │                                                                      │
Death / PTD Event                                                          │
    │                                                                      │
    ▼                                                                      │
Claims Intake Service (FNOL)                                               │
    │ claimant = nominee                                                   │
    │ documents = death cert + KYC + loan details                          │
    │                                                                      │
    ▼                                                                      │
Document Verification Service                                              │
    │ death cert verification via DigiLocker / municipal authority API     │
    │                                                                      │
    ▼                                                                      │
Loan Outstanding Calculator ◄──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
    │ fetches loan outstanding as of date of death
    │ sum insured = outstanding principal + accrued interest
    │ (reducing coverage — not the original loan amount)
    │
    ▼
Adjudication Engine
    │ coverage check (within policy term, premium paid, no exclusions)
    │ exclusions: suicide within first year, pre-existing illness (if applicable)
    │
    ▼
Settlement
    │ payee = NBFC (not the nominee)
    │ amount = outstanding loan principal + interest as of death date
    │ loan marked as 'settled_by_insurance' in loan service
    │ nominee notified — receives no cash but is relieved of loan obligation
    │
    ▼
Loan Service: close loan account; issue NOC to nominee
```

```typescript
// claims/loan-protection/lp-claim.service.ts
@Injectable()
export class LoanProtectionClaimService {
  async processLpClaim(
    loanId: string,
    eventType: 'death' | 'ptd',
    eventDate: Date,
    documents: FnolDocument[],
  ): Promise<LpClaimResult> {
    // Fetch loan and associated group policy
    const loan = await this.loanRepo.findById(loanId);
    const policy = await this.policyRepo.findByLoanId(loanId);

    // Calculate outstanding as of event date (not today — loan doesn't accrue post-death)
    const outstanding = await this.loanCalculator.getOutstandingAsOf(loanId, eventDate);

    // Register claim
    const claim = await this.fnolService.processFnol({
      policyNumber: policy.policyNumber,
      lossType: eventType === 'death' ? 'death' : 'disability',
      lossDate: eventDate,
      estimatedLossAmount: outstanding.totalOutstanding,
      claimantName: loan.nomineeDetails.name,
      claimantRelationship: loan.nomineeDetails.relationship,
      claimantContact: loan.nomineeDetails.mobile,
      initialDocuments: documents,
      channel: 'api',        // automated trigger from loan system
    });

    // Verify death certificate via DigiLocker API (if digital cert available)
    const deathCert = documents.find(d => d.type === 'death_certificate');
    if (deathCert?.digiLockerUri) {
      const verified = await this.digiLockerService.verify(deathCert.digiLockerUri);
      await this.documentService.markVerified(deathCert.id, verified);
    }

    return {
      claimNumber: claim.claimNumber,
      loanId,
      outstandingAmount: outstanding.totalOutstanding,
      status: 'registered',
      expectedSettlementDate: addDays(new Date(), 30),
    };
  }

  async settleToNbfc(claimId: string, approvedAmount: number): Promise<void> {
    const claim = await this.claimsRepo.findById(claimId);
    const loan = await this.loanRepo.findByClaimId(claimId);

    // Settlement goes to NBFC's trust account — not the nominee
    await this.settlementService.initiateSettlement(claimId, approvedAmount, 'system');

    // Mark loan as insurance-settled (suppress EMI collection, dunning)
    await this.loanRepo.update(loan.id, {
      status: 'settled_by_insurance',
      insuranceClaimId: claimId,
      insuranceSettledAmount: approvedAmount,
      insuranceSettledAt: new Date(),
    });

    // Stop NACH/recurring EMI collection immediately
    await this.emCollectionService.cancelFutureEmis(loan.id, 'insurance_settled');

    // Generate NOC for nominee
    await this.nocService.generateAndDispatch(loan.id, claim.claimantId);
  }
}
```

**Q14. "A health insurance cashless claim is rejected by the TPA at pre-authorization. The hospital is demanding payment from the patient. How do you handle this operationally and in the system?"**
A: Cashless claim rejection at pre-authorization is one of the most distressing insurance scenarios — the patient is in a hospital, potentially mid-treatment, and suddenly faces a large out-of-pocket expense. The system must handle this through a defined escalation and reimbursement pathway.

**The flow and system handling:**

```typescript
// claims/health/cashless-preauth.service.ts
@Injectable()
export class CashlessPreAuthService {
  async handlePreAuthRejection(
    preAuthId: string,
    rejectionReason: PreAuthRejectionReason,
    tpaRejectionDetails: TpaRejectionDetails,
  ): Promise<PreAuthRejectionHandling> {
    const preAuth = await this.preAuthRepo.findById(preAuthId);
    const policy = await this.policyRepo.findById(preAuth.policyId);

    await this.preAuthRepo.update(preAuthId, {
      status: 'rejected',
      rejectionReason,
      tpaRejectionCode: tpaRejectionDetails.code,
      tpaRejectionMessage: tpaRejectionDetails.message,
      rejectedAt: new Date(),
    });

    // Immediate notifications
    await this.notificationService.sendMultiChannel(preAuth.policyHolderId, {
      channels: ['sms', 'whatsapp', 'push'],
      template: 'cashless_preauth_rejected',
      data: {
        rejectionReason: this.getCustomerFriendlyReason(rejectionReason),
        reimbursementPath: `${this.config.portalUrl}/claims/reimbursement/${preAuth.id}`,
        helplineNumber: this.config.healthHelpline,
        escalationPath: 'Grievance can be filed at grievance@insurer.com',
      },
    });

    // Determine if rejection is contestable
    const isContestable = this.isRejectionContestable(rejectionReason);

    if (isContestable) {
      // Trigger internal review — TPA rejections can be overridden by insurer
      const escalation = await this.escalationService.create({
        type: 'cashless_preauth_override_request',
        preAuthId,
        priority: 'urgent',          // patient is in hospital — urgent
        assignedTo: 'medical_team',
        slaHours: 2,                 // 2-hour resolution SLA for in-patient rejections
        context: {
          patientCondition: preAuth.diagnosisCode,
          hospitalName: preAuth.hospitalName,
          estimatedAmount: preAuth.requestedAmount,
        },
      });

      return {
        outcome: 'escalated_for_override',
        escalationId: escalation.id,
        message: 'We are reviewing the rejection. You will hear from us within 2 hours.',
      };
    }

    // Non-contestable rejection — convert to reimbursement path
    // Patient pays hospital; files reimbursement claim post-discharge
    const reimbursementClaim = await this.convertToReimbursement(preAuth);

    return {
      outcome: 'converted_to_reimbursement',
      reimbursementClaimNumber: reimbursementClaim.claimNumber,
      message: 'Please pay the hospital and submit bills for reimbursement. ' +
               'Your claim has been pre-registered.',
    };
  }

  private isRejectionContestable(reason: PreAuthRejectionReason): boolean {
    // Contestable: coverage query, documentation gap, TPA error
    // Non-contestable: policy lapse, clear exclusion, waiting period
    const CONTESTABLE = new Set([
      'coverage_query',
      'documentation_incomplete',
      'tpa_system_error',
      'pre_existing_query',    // PED queries can often be resolved with records
    ]);
    return CONTESTABLE.has(reason);
  }
}
```

## Quick Reference Table

| Concept | Definition | Engineering Implication |
|---|---|---|
| FNOL | First Notice of Loss — starts claims clock | Policy validation + coverage check + duplicate check at intake |
| Triage | Initial classification into fast/standard/complex track | Rules engine based on amount, loss type, fraud score, completeness |
| STP | Straight-Through Processing — fully automated adjudication | Fast-track claims; no human reviewer; target < 4 hours |
| Document checklist | Product-specific mandatory and conditional document list | Rules engine per loss type; completeness check starts SLA clock |
| Adjudication | Coverage evaluation + payable amount calculation | BRE applying exclusions, deductibles, depreciation, sub-limits |
| Surveyor | IRDAI-licensed professional — mandatory for claims > ₹75,000 | License validation before assignment; 30-day report SLA |
| TPA | Third Party Administrator — manages health cashless claims | Pre-auth workflow; hospital network; medical officer review |
| Room rent proportionate deduction | If patient in higher room category, all charges proportionately reduced | Sub-limit engine; most disputed health claim deduction |
| Depreciation | IRDAI schedule applied to replaced motor parts | Age-driven rates; rubber/tyre 50%; glass 0% |
| SLA breach | IRDAI mandates 30-day non-investigated, 45-day investigated | SLA monitor cron; 2% p.a. interest penalty on delays |
| Fraud score | Composite risk score from multiple signals at FNOL and adjudication | Indicators: claim frequency, early claim, geo-inconsistency, doc mismatch |
| Cashless claim | Hospital paid directly by insurer via TPA | Pre-auth required; rejection → reimbursement conversion pathway |
| Reimbursement | Patient pays; insurer reimburses post-discharge | Standard document collection and adjudication flow |
| CoI | Certificate of Insurance — proof of coverage | Required at FNOL to validate policy |
| NPA trigger | For credit life insurance: missed EMI → claim if death/PTD | Loan system integration; settlement goes to NBFC |
| Settlement interest | 2% p.a. above RBI rate on delayed settlement | Automated calculation on SLA breach; reduces insurer profitability |

**Tip for the interview:** At the 5 YOE level, claims management questions appear in three forms: system design ("design a claims management system for embedded insurance"), domain knowledge ("what is the IRDAI 30-day settlement mandate and how does it shape your architecture?"), and debugging/operational scenarios ("a cashless pre-authorization was rejected while the patient is admitted — what do you do?"). The senior differentiator is demonstrating that you understand the **regulatory and financial precision** behind every engineering decision — that the 30-day SLA is not a product goal but a statutory obligation with an automatic interest penalty, that depreciation rates are not configurable but fixed by IRDAI regulation, that room rent proportionate deduction is the most legally contested clause in health insurance and your system must apply it exactly as the policy terms state, and that fraud scoring at FNOL is not a nice-to-have but a financial necessity given the 10–15% fraud rate in Indian insurance. Interviewers at insurtech companies, health insurance platforms, and embedded insurance providers will probe whether you understand the TPA model in health, the surveyor mandate in motor, and why a credit life claim pays the NBFC rather than the nominee. That specificity is the marker of a fintech-domain engineer versus a backend generalist.
